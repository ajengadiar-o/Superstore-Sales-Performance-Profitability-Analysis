# Superstore Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Superstore sales and profitability performance using Microsoft Excel. The analysis focuses on sales trends, product sub-category performance, profitability, cost distribution, and discount-related patterns to identify business growth opportunities and areas that require improvement.

The project demonstrates how Excel can be used to prepare transactional data, perform exploratory analysis, summarize business metrics, and communicate findings through structured reports and dashboards.

## Business Objectives

- Evaluate sales and profitability performance across product sub-categories and reporting periods.
- Identify products with strong growth and those experiencing declining sales.
- Examine cost, discount, and profitability patterns to support business performance evaluation.
- Provide data-driven recommendations for improving sales performance and cost efficiency.

## Tools & Methods

**Primary Tool:** Microsoft Excel

- Data preparation and cleaning
- Data type validation and missing-value checks
- Duplicate checks
- Feature engineering and calculated columns
- PivotTables and summary analysis
- Sales trend and sub-category performance analysis
- Dashboard development and data visualization

## Key Insights

- Sales growth varied across product sub-categories rather than increasing consistently across the product portfolio.
- Appliances and Copiers showed strong growth, while Envelopes and Bookcases experienced declines during the analyzed periods.
- Examining cost distribution, discounts, and profitability helps identify areas requiring further investigation and potential operational improvements.

## Business Recommendations

- Review sales strategies for declining sub-categories such as Envelopes and Bookcases.
- Investigate the factors contributing to growth in Appliances and Copiers and assess whether successful strategies can be applied to other categories.
- Monitor discounts, costs, and profit margins together rather than evaluating sales revenue alone.
- Use periodic Excel reports and dashboards to track business performance and identify changes in sales trends.
